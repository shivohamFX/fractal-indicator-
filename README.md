# Fractal Indicator

A lightweight fractal-based trading indicator designed to help identify market structure, swing points, and potential areas of interest on the chart based on ttfm.

## Features

- Fractal / swing high and low detection
- Market structure visualization
- Clean and minimalist chart display
- Designed for discretionary technical analysis
- Open-source TradingView implementation

 Usage

1. Open TradingView.
2. Add the indicator to your chart.
3. Adjust the available settings according to your trading style.
4. Use the identified fractals and market structure as part of your analysis.

 Disclaimer

This indicator is provided for educational and research purposes only. It is not financial advice. Always perform your own analysis and risk management before making trading decisions.
